At signup, say what you're actually training for, in your own words, and put a small stake on the line through your Cult.fit membership.

It watches quietly

Most days, nothing happens. You show up, your stake stays yours, and Gritfit stays out of the way.

03

If you drift, it speaks up

Not a push notification you'll swipe away. One short call, in your own cloned voice, reminding you what you said on day one.

Your money is a promise, not a punishment.

  • It's a hold, not a charge. Your stake is pre-authorized, not deducted — nothing moves unless you genuinely go quiet.
  • One miss is never the trigger. Gritfit only steps in when a miss lines up with a real pattern, not on a single off day.
  • You always get a chance to recommit first. The call comes before the stake does — recommitting on the spot releases the hold.
  • Forfeits are rare by design. They only happen if the reminder itself goes unanswered, not if you simply miss a session.
